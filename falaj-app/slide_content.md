## Slide 2: The Problem We Solve
**Heading:** UAE Agriculture Faces Critical Challenges
**Key Points:**
- 80% of UAE food is imported — local production must increase urgently
- Water scarcity: UAE receives less than 100mm rainfall annually, making every drop precious
- 40% of irrigation water is wasted due to outdated farming methods and lack of real-time data
- Small farmers lack access to modern technology, market connections, and data-driven insights
- Government needs real-time visibility into agricultural performance across the nation
**Visual:** Split view — arid desert landscape on one side, thriving green farm on the other
